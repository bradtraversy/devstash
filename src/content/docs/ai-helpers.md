DevStash has four AI helpers. They are free for everyone for now, up to 20 requests an hour.

## Suggest Tags

In the New Item form and in the drawer's Edit mode, **Suggest Tags** proposes tags from the item's title and content. Accept or dismiss each one.

## Describe

Next to the Description field, **Describe** writes a one or two sentence description of the item for you to keep or edit.

## Explain

Open a snippet or command in the drawer and click **Explain** in the code header. The explanation opens in a tab next to the code.

## Optimize

Open a prompt in the drawer and click **Optimize**. The rewritten prompt opens in a tab next to the original, and **Use This** saves it over the original.

## What gets sent

The helpers send the item's title, type, language, the URL for links, and up to 2,000 characters of its content to OpenAI to generate the result. Nothing is sent until you click a helper.
