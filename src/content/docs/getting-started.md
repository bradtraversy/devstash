DevStash is one place for the snippets, commands, prompts, notes, and links you keep reaching for, and a quick way to share any of them by link. Everything you save is private until you share it.

## Create an account

Sign up with an email and password, or continue with GitHub.

## Save from the paste box

Home opens with a paste box. Paste anything and DevStash guesses what it is:

- **Snippet**: code, with the language detected for highlighting
- **Command**: one or more terminal commands; a leading `$ ` prompt is dropped
- **Note**: prose or markdown, including a whole doc or gist (see [Notes as pages](/docs/notes-as-pages))
- **Prompt**: a request or instructions for an AI model
- **Link**: a single web URL

If the guess is wrong, pick another type from the chips under the box before saving. **Save** keeps the item private. **Save and share** saves it, makes it unlisted, and copies its link (see [Sharing and links](/docs/sharing)).

For the full form with a title, description, language, tags, and collections, use **New Item** in the top bar.

## Find things again

- The sidebar lists Home, Shared, Favorites, your item types, and your collections.
- Any list switches between dense rows and code cards, and shows 25, 50, or 100 items per page.
- Pinned items stay at the top of Home. Favorites collects everything you star.
- Search opens with Cmd or Ctrl+K and matches item titles, the start of their content, and collection names.

Click any item to open it in the drawer, where you can edit, copy, pin, favorite, share, or delete it.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Cmd or Ctrl+K | Open search |
| Cmd or Ctrl+Enter | Save from the paste box |
| Cmd or Ctrl+Shift+Enter | Save and share from the paste box |
| Esc | Close the drawer, a dialog, or search |

## Make it yours

Settings has the code editor preferences (font size, tab size, word wrap, minimap, and theme), your public handle, your password, and your data (see [Import and export](/docs/import-export)).
